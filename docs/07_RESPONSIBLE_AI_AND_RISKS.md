# Responsible AI and Research Risks

## Bias and representativeness

The scoring framework may favor companies that disclose more AI activity, especially large-cap technology companies.

## Robustness

Backtesting financial predictions carries overfitting risk. Use out-of-sample validation, walk-forward tests, and sensitivity analysis where the data volume permits.

## LLM extraction reliability

LLM outputs may vary by prompt, model, source quality, and industry language. Store prompt versions, model versions, evidence, and confidence.

## Misuse

This system is a decision-support and research tool. It is not an automated trading system and should not be presented as investment advice.

## Human-in-the-loop

Company-level conclusions should show evidence and allow human review before external publication.
